Try an example

Each example is a realistic study with simulated data, so the true answer is known. Open one to see the prompt, download the data, and compare the results with the truth.

20 examples are DONE: every quantity the report interprets passed planted truth at the example's design. 9 are OPEN: at least one did not, and the page says which results can still be used. OPEN examples are kept on purpose; they show how CascadiaTools tells you when something can't be trusted. See the verdict table.
